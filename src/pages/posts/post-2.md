#Spindle

##description 

These gangly creatures are some of the biggest in the game, at least in terms of height. They're virtually impossible to ignore: not only are they massive by they generate an incessant chattering noise when nearby. They are significantly more durable to weapons like the pistol, but explosives like grenades or red barrels can instantly kill them. Although they can be harder to avoid in tight quarters, Spindles are not all that aggressive, but if provoked their long gait can make it hard to stand your ground.
