# GUEST


## description

This is the more common type of creature found in the park. They are similar to typical zombies, as they are generally humanoid (with some variance between specimens) they are slow, clumsy, and can only attack with their hands. They are reasonably durable, but their slow movement makes it relatively easy to execute headshots on them. Depending on size of the space they occupy, they can easily be avoided; if not, they are easily dispatched with the shotgun or a few careful shots from the 9mm pistol.
