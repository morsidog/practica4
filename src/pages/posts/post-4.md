#GRINNER

##description

Similar to the Guest, these monsters are bipedal but have otherwise begun to lose their overall human shape: they no longer have arms. They are covered in spikes and, when they die, explode into a shower of shrapnel, making it important to take them out from a reasonable distance or wait for them to explode before approaching.
